# Desirable Inter-Feeds (Pvt) Ltd - Corporate Portal & Contract Farming SLA Generator

A static, enterprise-grade corporate portal and client-side contract generation system for **Desirable Inter-Feeds (Pvt) Ltd** (Rusape & Harare, Zimbabwe).

Deployable with zero server backend dependencies (100% free on **Cloudflare Pages**, GitHub Pages, or any static host).

## 🌾 Overview

Desirable Inter-Feeds (Pvt) Ltd is an integrated agro-industrial conglomerate operating across five core statutory divisions:
1. **Agri-Feeds, Milling & Farming Operations**: Industrial stock feed milling (poultry, swine, cattle), maize meal processing, food packaging, and commercial farming.
2. **Professional Advisory, Agronomy & Media**: Actuarial risk assessment, agricultural consultancy, agronomists, nutritionists, PR, media filming, and conferencing.
3. **Commercial Trading, Livestock Dealing & Brokerage**: Car sales, vehicle dealership, auctioneering, livestock trading, real estate, and insurance brokerage.
4. **Logistics, Transport, Energy & Earthmoving**: Heavy road freight haulage, nationwide fleet charter, commercial borehole drilling, and solar PV energy systems.
5. **Civil Engineering, Construction & Metal Fabrication**: Road surfacing, service stations, industrial warehouses, automated sliding gates, and perimeter fencing.

## 📄 Contract Farming & Diaspora Support SLA Generator

The portal features an in-browser agreement generator that allows prospective contract farmers and diaspora clients to:
* Fill out client, farm, and caretaker credentials.
* Select feed formulations and specify target batch quantities.
* Choose payment arrangement options (Direct Settlement, 60/40 Split Scheme, Pre-Harvest Facility).
* Specify collateral and asset pledges.
* Provide digital signature and execution dates.
* **Instant Client-Side PDF Generation**: Uses `pdf-lib` to overlay inputs onto the official vector agreement template (`Desirable_Interfeeds_Fillable_Form.pdf`) with embedded Base64 fallback, working 100% offline or on static hosting without server execution.
